# Aniimo Wiki media inventory — 2026-10-06

Scope: the currently published `AI-Road-X/AnvilWiki` source tree for `aniimo-guides.com`. This is an asset inventory, not a legal opinion or proof that future uploads are licensed.

## Findings

- All 212 MDX articles (106 per language) have no `image:` or `gallery:` frontmatter, Markdown image embeds, or inline `<img>` tags. The current article bodies do not republish official Aniimo character artwork or screenshots.
- `public/images/hero.webp` and the branded favicon PNG/SVG set were generated by the repository's `scripts/gen-assets.ts` from the site's name, initial, and brand colors. `public/images/hero.svg` is likewise a text-and-gradient site card. The 192×192 and 512×512 icons referenced by the web manifest are present in `public/`.
- `public/favicon.ico` remains a legacy template asset, but the live layout points browsers to the generated SVG/PNG icons. It is not evidence of game-art usage.
- The templates can display YouTube thumbnails or article gallery images if future content supplies them. No current content/configuration supplies video IDs or gallery images. Recheck the source, attribution, and display rights before enabling those paths or adding game screenshots.

## Decision

For the **current deployed article imagery**, AD-19 can be treated as passed: no third-party game images were found. This does not resolve separate questions about text originality, game-name trademark use, or rights in future media. Do not add unlicensed art merely to make articles look fuller.
