## Where is the redemption menu?

Public launch coverage reports that codes are entered from the settings area reached through the main phone menu. The in-game mailbox is used for rewards after completing **Chasing The Clouds** with Dr. Lewis; this specific unlock condition is third-party reporting, not a published official quest rule. See the [mailbox unlock and missing-reward checks](/quests/chasing-the-clouds/) for details.

Codes are case-sensitive. Enter them exactly as shown and check the [Aniimo codes list](/codes/getting-started/) for source and status before using one.
For rewards sent through events rather than a redeemable code, see the [official launch rewards guide](/guides/official-launch-rewards/).

## If the reward does not arrive

1. Recheck capitalization and extra spaces, and confirm the code is still current.
2. Confirm the mailbox is unlocked and open the reward message to claim it.
3. If an accepted reward has not appeared after five minutes, PC Gamer's third-party launch guide suggests fast traveling to another area; if needed, restart and check the inbox again.
4. Check the linked announcement or official notices for expiration or a service delay.

Never provide an account password or download an unknown file just to redeem a code.
