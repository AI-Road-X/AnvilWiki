## Where is the redemption menu?

Public launch coverage reports that codes are entered from the settings area reached through the main phone menu. The in-game mailbox is used for rewards after completing **Chasing The Clouds** with Dr. Lewis.

Codes are case-sensitive. Enter them exactly as shown and check the [Aniimo codes list](/codes/getting-started/) for source and status before using one.

## If the reward does not arrive

1. Recheck capitalization, spaces, and server region.
2. Open the in-game mailbox and wait a few minutes.
3. Fast travel to another area and check again.
4. Restart the game and review official notices for delays or expiration.

Never provide an account password or download an unknown file just to redeem a code.
