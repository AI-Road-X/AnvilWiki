## Where do Aniimo codes come from?

Codes should be traceable to Aniimo's official website, official social channels, or in-game announcements. To make the evidence clear, this page separates independently verified codes from codes that have only been reported by outside guides; a report is not a promise that a code will work for your account.

Several gaming guides report that `aniimazingfroyo` grants 20 Glimmer on the America/US server. Pocket Tactics added it on October 9, while Dexerto and Beebom also identify the regional restriction. **We have not redeemed it in-game**, and we have not found a matching official announcement, so its current availability and account eligibility remain unconfirmed by this site. Treat it as a reported candidate, not a guaranteed active code. Check your server before trying it; do not change regions or use a third-party service to claim it.

## What should I check before redeeming a code?

Codes may be case-sensitive and time-limited. Copy the spelling exactly, remove any extra spaces, and check the listed status before trying it. If a code fails, review the game's official notices and confirm that it has not expired.

Never share your account password or download an unknown file to claim a code. For redemption steps and basic troubleshooting, see [how to redeem Aniimo codes](/codes/how-to-redeem/).

## What can I claim if no code is verified here?

A redeem code is different from a launch-event reward. Check our [official launch rewards guide](/guides/official-launch-rewards/) and the [launch-day checklist](/guides/launch-day-checklist/) for ways to review in-game mail and event notices. Do not enter your password on a third-party “free gift” page.

If you find a code in an official announcement, follow the [redemption and troubleshooting steps](/codes/how-to-redeem/). A failed attempt can depend on timing, region, or account eligibility; the official notice is the final reference.
