## How do you compare one Aniimo upgrade without guessing a tier?

Choose one problem and one proposed change, then write down the conditions that must stay fixed. This page is a worksheet for a decision you can check, not another overall tier list. The [role shortlist](/tier-list/getting-started/) identifies candidates when you have not yet chosen a function.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | One observable issue in one named activity |
| Current option / candidate | Creature and exact form |
| Conditions held fixed | Team, target, level, equipment and game version |
| Published difference | Same-field attributes or explicit skill conditions |
| Observation | What actually happened; leave blank before a test |
| Resource trade-off | Costs, duration and recovery restrictions |
| Decision | Keep, investigate, or defer; explain why |

Use private notes for account-specific details. A proposed test is not evidence that it was completed.

## What does a filled BREAK comparison look like?

Comparing [Geodeback Basic](/characters/geodeback/) with [Blazen Basic](/characters/blazen/) illustrates why a larger total is not the whole decision. Their own sourced references provide the following fields; the observations column is intentionally not filled with invented results.

| Field | Geodeback Basic | Blazen Basic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published total / BREAK | 486 / 102 | 530 / 104 |
| Relevant mechanic | Earth Affinity during Tunneling | Power Sustain / Overcharged |
| Condition to check | Underground state and sufficient stamina | Six critical hits from skills |
| Resource or vulnerability | Tunnel drains stamina; Pound can launch it | Six-second state; no UP gain |
| Observed BREAK performance | Not measured here | Not measured here |

The attribute differences are **44 in total** and **2 in BREAK**. Neither is a measured damage gain. A useful next observation is whether the relevant state is active during the window you care about. If you cannot reproduce that condition, a permanent-buff estimate would misdescribe the comparison.

## How do you use the worksheet for a healing problem?

For [Bulbly](/characters/bulbly/), write down recipient element, position and whether the beam is channeled. A teammate not meeting the published target condition is a different problem from an eligible teammate who receives no effect. Keep the player-controlled cast separate from the retreat clone.

For a contrasting Support choice, [Cozite](/characters/cozite/) supplies a resistance debuff and smoke targeting effect, not a documented heal. Replacing one with the other solely because both say Support would change the function being tested. First decide which function is actually missing.

## When should you defer the decision?

Defer a claim of superiority when you cannot keep the activity or form comparable, do not know the acquisition cost, or have not observed the relevant result. You can still use a creature you enjoy; the uncertainty only limits what this page can prove about an upgrade.

For reported rankings elsewhere, check whether their tested activity matches yours and whether their evidence explains a different result. Do not average incompatible letter grades. Our [verification notes](/characters/verification-notes/) explain the source-versus-observation distinction. An unfilled test field is more useful than a fabricated best-build answer.
