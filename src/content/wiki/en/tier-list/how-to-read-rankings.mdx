## What makes an Aniimo tier list useful?

A useful Aniimo tier list states which game version and activity it covers, explains how placements were decided, and separates confirmed facts from opinion. No single ranking answers every player's question. Before acting on a grade, check the list's scope and whether its evidence supports the recommendation.

## Use this five-point checklist

1. **Version and date:** Is it clear when the ranking was checked, and which game version it describes?
2. **Activity:** Does it distinguish the goal being ranked, such as progression, battles, or collection, instead of implying one universal order?
3. **Criteria:** Are the reasons for placements explained, or are there only unexplained letters and numbers?
4. **Evidence:** Can factual claims be cross-checked against the [character compendium](/characters/), [official type reference](/characters/official-aniimo-types/), or [verification notes](/characters/verification-notes/)?
5. **Your resources:** Does the recommendation fit what you can obtain and invest? Check the [catch-tool guide](/items/official-catch-tools/) and relevant [quest entries](/quests/) before spending limited resources.

If a list omits its version, activity, or reasoning, treat its placements as unverified opinion—not an official ranking. For the wider site policy on character placements, see the [Aniimo tier list overview](/tier-list/getting-started/).
