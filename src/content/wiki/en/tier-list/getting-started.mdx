## Which Aniimo should you compare for your team's missing role?

Start with the problem, then compare a small set of candidates that can address it. A missing heal, unreliable BREAK window and low damage are different problems. The table gives documented options to investigate; it does not say these are the five best creatures or that other choices are excluded.

| Need | Candidate and form | Relevant published condition | First check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing for a Lightning teammate | [Bulbly, Basic](/characters/bulbly/) | Lightning Discharge heals eligible teammates in range while channeled | Teammate element, position and channel |
| Earth Resistance debuff | [Cozite, Basic](/characters/cozite/) | Skills or Ultimate must hit to apply Potent Erosion | A registered hit and visible debuff |
| BREAK while underground | [Geodeback, Basic](/characters/geodeback/) | Earth Affinity is tied to Tunneling | State, stamina and incoming Pound actions |
| BREAK with an EP-saving window | [Blazen, Basic](/characters/blazen/) | Power Sustain needs skill critical hits; Overcharged prevents UP gain | Trigger reliability and UP cost |
| Damage against stacked Fire Debuff | [Infergon, Basic](/characters/infergon/) | Power of Fire needs more than five stacks | Whether the target actually has six or more |

## Which listed stats can you compare directly?

The same field can be compared across the same named forms as a reference. Different attributes should not be converted into one invented performance score. HP attributes are not final combat HP, and REGEN is not a published healing-per-second measurement.

| Basic Form | Total | HP | BREAK | ATK | REGEN |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bulbly | 404 | 83 | 37 | 68 | 90 |
| Cozite | 448 | 77 | 50 | 87 | 100 |
| Geodeback | 486 | 99 | 102 | 81 | 70 |
| Blazen | 530 | 100 | 104 | 100 | 86 |
| Infergon | 505 | 95 | 50 | 125 | 80 |

The order follows the functions above, not strength. A total of 530 does not establish a better healer than a total of 404. No investment cost, availability or full damage formula is included in this table.

## How do you turn this shortlist into one decision?

1. Write one observable problem: for example, “my Lightning teammate is not receiving healing,” not “my team is bad.”
2. Open the corresponding reference and check the named form and targeting rule.
3. Keep your activity, team and equipment constant when comparing one change.
4. Record the effect and resource trade-off; leave acquisition or build-cost fields unknown if you lack evidence.
5. Use the [single-upgrade worksheet](/tier-list/how-to-read-rankings/) before committing scarce resources.

For BREAK comparisons, note that Geodeback's stated 20% extra BREAK needs the underground state, while Blazen's cost reduction occurs in a six-second state with a UP restriction. Neither condition is interchangeable with an always-on benefit. For Infergon, five stacks do not meet a “more than five” requirement.

## Why does this page not assign S, A or B tiers?

A common ranking would need comparable tests, a defined activity and rules for equipment, forms and investment. Those measurements are not available here. Publishing letters anyway would replace missing evidence with confidence, not help the player choose.

This page therefore supplies a concrete role shortlist, numerical references and decision steps. Use the [type guide](/characters/official-aniimo-types/) when a role or element label is unclear. This is narrower than a universal “best team,” but you can verify each stated condition.
