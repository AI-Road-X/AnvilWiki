## Is there one best Aniimo tier list?

No single ranking is reliable for every player or activity. A useful Aniimo tier list must explain its game version, what it measures, and the evidence behind each placement. This guide does not assign character tiers without comparable, verified performance data; use the checks below before spending scarce resources.

## Check what the ranking measures

Start by identifying the activity: a character that helps with exploration may not be the best choice for a particular battle or collection goal. A list should state its use case rather than present one unexplained overall grade. Compare the relevant [character compendium](/characters/) entries and their documented abilities before treating a placement as advice.

## Check the evidence and date

Prefer rankings that show their game version, update date, testing conditions, and reasons for each placement. Separate confirmed facts from player opinion. If the author does not explain how characters were compared, treat the list as an opinion rather than a verified result. Our [verification notes](/characters/verification-notes/) explain how this site distinguishes sourced details from unconfirmed claims.

## Match the choice to your resources

Acquisition difficulty and investment cost matter as much as a letter grade. Check the available [catch tools](/items/official-catch-tools/) and [quest guides](/quests/) before committing scarce items or changing your progression plan. Revisit a ranking after a game update only when there is new evidence that affects the comparison.

For a quick review checklist, see [how to read an Aniimo tier list](/tier-list/how-to-read-rankings/). Until comparable performance evidence is available, avoid treating any unsupported character order as an official or universal ranking.
