## How should you look up an Aniimo item?

Use the [items index](/items/) to find the item entry, then check what the page actually confirms about its use. Keep currencies, progression materials, and capture consumables separate: they serve different purposes and should not be compared as if they were interchangeable.

## Which item references are useful?

The guide to [currency and catch items](/items/currency-and-catch-items/) groups common resource questions, while the [official catch-tools reference](/items/official-catch-tools/) focuses on capture equipment. Check the relevant entry before spending limited resources, and do not assume an item's effect or availability from its name alone.

## How can you avoid outdated advice?

Look for an update date and a source for claims about item effects or acquisition. If a page does not document a method, treat that method as unknown rather than filling the gap with guesswork. For capture decisions, compare the item information with the relevant [character compendium](/characters/) and [quest guides](/quests/) so you have the right context.
