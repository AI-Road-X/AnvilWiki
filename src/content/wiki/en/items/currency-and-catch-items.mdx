## A few resource categories

Public launch coverage describes **Glimmer** as a currency used for useful exploration and progression items, while **Primegy** is associated with daily stamina-style spending. Aniipod variants can affect capture results or potential quality.

Prices, drops, and uses may change between versions. This wiki separates reported uses from in-game values that have been verified.

## How to spend carefully

Keep items tied to quests, events, or limited acquisition until their purpose is clear. When you obtain rare capture items such as an Aniipod Ultra, Sparkling Cube, or Prismana Orb, confirm the target and version before spending it.
