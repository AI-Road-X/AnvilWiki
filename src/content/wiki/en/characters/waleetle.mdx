The official Prismana description says absorbing Prismana energy turns Waleetle's speed-chasing instinct into fiery power and its Aniipod Stone into molten lava. The official page also lists Basic and Snowfield forms; all stats and skills below are specifically from the [official Prismana Form entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/id/item/057/prismana-form), not a claim that every form shares these values.

## Mobility and trait

- **Hustle:** Enters Hustle state, increasing movement speed by consuming stamina.
- **Overloaded Engine:** Movement and skill use build Overload Energy. When full, the next skill deals 40% extra damage and immediately brings Sandstorm Spin to maximum speed.
