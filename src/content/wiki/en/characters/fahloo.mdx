Fahloo rubs oil over its body to create thick foam that cleans and protects it, although it may slip on the foam. The official Erlath description says Erlath's dense, soft bubbles protect the Fahloos riding on its back; see the [Erlath compendium](/characters/erlath/).

## Trait and support role

- **Bubble:** Every 12 seconds Fahloo enters Bathing; its next skill creates a puddle.
- **Floaty Bubble:** Rubs itself to create a large bubble around itself.

Its listed skills combine area movement and EP control with a bubble projectile that pushes targets away.

## Fahloo or Erlath: what does the published kit actually show?

Both are Water/REGEN Aniimo with the Bubble trait, Floaty Bubble mobility, and Tideblossom Coast listed as a habitat. The official pages nevertheless give them different support tools:

| Reader question | Fahloo No.080 | [Erlath No.081](/characters/erlath/) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which movement levels are listed? | Fly Lv.1; Swim Lv.1 | Fly Lv.3; Swim Lv.2 |
| What does the bubble support skill do? | Slows nearby targets and their EP recovery, while speeding up allies | Has a comparable movement/EP-recovery effect; also lists a separate bubble field that restores teammates' EP |
| Is a Water-damage team buff listed? | Not in the three skills shown on the Basic Form page | Crazy Bubbles lists +16% teammate Water damage for 30 seconds |

The **REGEN** role label alone does not prove that Fahloo directly restores teammates' EP. Its published Basic Form skills show *enemy EP-recovery reduction*, not the EP-restoring field described for Erlath. This comparison is about documented effects, not a power ranking or a claim that one replaces the other in every team.
