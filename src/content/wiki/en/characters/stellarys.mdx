The official description says that Stellarys prefer solitude and often move through the night. Their starry, cloak-like forms resemble mysterious mages, and they can teleport using psychic energy. The official page lists Basic Form, Rainstorm Form, and Prismana Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Fly Lv.1**: supports basic flying traversal.
- **Grand Sorcerer**: for 5 seconds after using a skill, basic-attack hits deal an additional 6 Might of damage.
