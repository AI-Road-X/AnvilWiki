## How does Punch Rush trigger?

Mark the target with Pinecone Bomb and spend that mark with Blazing Fist Barrage to prime Punch Rush on your next basic attack. Heavy Tail Strike also spends the mark, so choose which move gets it and apply another mark for the other move.

Blazing Fist Barrage costs 30 and has listed power 85. On a marked hit it primes the next basic attack, whose Punch Rush sequence is described as dealing 60 Might. The skill's listed power and the follow-up's Might describe different actions.

Choose Barrage when you want to set up the follow-up. Choose Heavy Tail Strike when you need its marked hit's launch and stun. Both consume the mark. Compared with Squarrel's 53 HP and 94 BREAK base attributes, Squashel lists 110 HP and 108 BREAK, plus Climb Lv.2 instead of Lv.1.
