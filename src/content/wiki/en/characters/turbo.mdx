## How do Shrouded in Mist stacks work?

- **Fly Lv.1** is the current listed traversal level on both checked pages.
- **Cloud Float** is the current mobility label.
- **Shrouded in Mist** gains a stack from a basic attack or after the published five-second off-field condition. Four stacks activate the state. Casting a skill consumes it for an enhanced effect.

Watch the stack and state indicator before deciding which skill to use. Basic attacks provide one route to the state, while the off-field wording gives another condition to check when rotating teammates. Select the effect your next action needs before using the skill that spends the state.

## Which enhanced skill should you choose?

Choose according to the next task. Nimbi Burst supports a team damage window, Nebula Burst specifically mentions Dark damage, and Cloud Shield protects Turbo itself. Each choice spends the available state, so line up the intended effect with the action you are about to take.

| Choice | Published effect to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Nimbi Burst | Team damage and BREAK efficiency rise 15% for 20 seconds. In the enhanced state, the boost reaches 30% and extends to nearby allies. |
| Nebula Burst | Party Dark damage rises 15% for 20 seconds. The enhanced state raises it to 30% and extends it to nearby allies. |
| Cloud Orb | The enhanced hit is described as 180% Might. |
| Thunder Bolt | The enhanced hit is described as 120% Might and applies five seconds of Paralysis. |
| Cloud Shield | Compare the self-shield and stamina conditions below. |

Before choosing, read your current equipped skills and the recipient of each effect. The [Luminelle support guide](/characters/luminelle/) uses the same target-first approach for a different support kit.

## How does the enhanced Cloud Shield differ?

The published shield applies to Turbo itself. Normal Cloud Shield absorbs up to 15% maximum HP and reduces stamina consumption by 15% for 20 seconds. In Shrouded in Mist, those values become 18% and the duration becomes 30 seconds. Use the current state indicator when comparing the two outcomes.

For a repeatable check, record whether the state is active, cast the shield, then observe its displayed duration and the next action's stamina use. Change one condition per attempt. If your actual problem is team survival, compare the recipients and activation conditions in [Panpanta's shield reference](/characters/panpanta/) before assigning a support role.

Collectible Drop lists seven pickups that each restore 6% HP when collected. Include the pickup step in your recovery check. For traversal planning, use Fly Lv.1 alongside the [regional references](/maps/official-region-names/) and the requirement shown by your current objective.
