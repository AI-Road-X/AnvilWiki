## Mobility, trait, and skills

- **Fly Lv.2** supports higher-level flying traversal.
- **Cloudwalk** lets Turbo float freely among clouds.
- **Shrouded in Mist** activates after 4 basic attacks; casting a skill consumes the state to enhance the skill.

| Skill | Type | Cost | Power |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Nimbi Burst | Magic | 10 | 30 |
| Cloud Orb | Magic | 10 | 30 |
| Thunder Bolt | Magic | 10 | 30 |
| Cloud Shield | Support | 20 | 0 |
| Collectible Drop | Support | 0 | 0 |
| ATK | Magic | 0 | 6 |

After four basic attacks, the next skill consumes **Shrouded in Mist**. This makes the choice of skill important: Nimbi Burst buffs teammates' damage and BREAK efficiency; Cloud Orb and Thunder Bolt gain larger explosions; Cloud Shield gains a stronger, longer-lasting shield and stamina benefit. Collectible Drop releases seven pickups, each restoring 6% HP. These are descriptions of the official skill text, not a tested team ranking.
