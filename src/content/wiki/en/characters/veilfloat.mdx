## Is Veilfloat a different kind of healer from Bulbly?

The two official Basic Form pages publish the same Lightning Discharge and Swift Retreat descriptions. Veilfloat has higher listed attributes in several fields, but identical wording is not a test of real healing output. Start by checking whether your target is eligible before deciding that a different support will fix the problem.

| Published Basic Form field | Veilfloat No.078 | Bulbly No.077 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total | 473 | 404 |
| HP | 99 | 83 |
| BREAK | 45 | 37 |
| ATK | 93 | 68 |
| M.DEF / P.DEF | 74 / 72 | 63 / 63 |
| REGEN | 90 | 90 |

The total differs by 69, while REGEN is unchanged. That does not make 69 a healing improvement or establish how reference HP becomes combat HP. The [Bulbly guide](/characters/bulbly/) explains the same targeting restriction.

## Why might a teammate still receive no healing?

Lightning Discharge heals Lightning-type teammates in range while the beam is channeled; the published rate is 2% HP each second. Holding the skill sustains it. Replacing Bulbly with Veilfloat does not, in the text, remove the element, range or channel condition. Check those before changing investment.

- Confirm the recipient's current element, not just the support's element.
- Keep the intended recipient within the skill's effective range.
- Check that channeling continues rather than ending after a tap.
- Compare the same activity and equipment before interpreting a different HP result.

The source does not make the percentage's scaling basis precise enough to calculate healing from the reference HP field. Multiplying 99 by 2% would confuse an attribute reference with a verified combat formula.

## Does the retreat clone replace the beam's healing?

Swift Retreat describes moving backward and leaving a clone that attacks using Lightning Discharge, with a 15% slow for ten seconds. It does not explicitly confirm a universal clone heal. Treat its attack and slow as the documented behavior, then separately verify any claimed healing interaction in the client.

The page lists cost/power 20/53 for Swift Retreat and 10/20 for Lightning Discharge. Those labels alone do not establish every ongoing channel-cost rule. Arc Surge also contains the same malformed “12%%” value seen on Bulbly's page; it is not used here as a reliable build multiplier.

## Where is Veilfloat listed, and how should you compare it?

The official page names Tideblossom Coast and the Glow mobility ability. This is a regional lead, not a guaranteed spawn, capture route or schedule.
