The official description says that Chirpi often hover at low altitudes, watching every movement on the beach and causing a ruckus. The official page lists Basic Form, Beach Form, and Highland Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Fly Lv.2**: supports higher-level flying traversal.
- **Band Member**: when switching Aniimo in battle, restores 20 EP to the next Chirpi family member switched in; it can trigger once every 20 seconds at most.
