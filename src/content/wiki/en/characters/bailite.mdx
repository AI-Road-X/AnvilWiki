The official description says Bailite's upper and lower body can rotate independently, helping it observe its surroundings and transform into a defensive turret. The official French-language entry uses the localized name Bastiroc; its data is linked below.

## Trait and combat role

- **Deep Erosion:** When a skill or Ultimate hits a target, its Earth Resistance is reduced by 2%, stacking up to 5 times for 20 seconds.

This trait rewards repeated skill hits and can support Rock/Earth damage from the team. The official page does not publish a separate mobility entry for this Basic Form.
