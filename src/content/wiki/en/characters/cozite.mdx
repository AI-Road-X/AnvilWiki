## Who is Aniimo 075, and what does Cozite add?

No.075 is **Cozite**, a Rock Support whose published tools change resistance and targeting. That is a different job from restoring a teammate's HP. Decide which problem you need to solve before using a Support label as if it meant every creature supplies the same effect.

## How does Potent Erosion stack?

A skill or Ultimate must **hit a target** to apply the trait's **2% Earth Resistance reduction for 20 seconds**, with up to **10 stacks**. The source does not explain whether subsequent hits refresh all stacks, only one stack, or use another timing rule.

<Image src={diagram} alt="Original Cozite reference: skill or Ultimate hits apply the stated resistance reduction with a ten-stack cap; Smoke Screen blocks aiming through smoke, not all damage." loading="lazy" />

Ten stacks of a resistance effect should not be rewritten as a guaranteed 20% increase in final damage. That would require the resistance formula, stacking rule and target conditions. A zero-power support effect also does not prove that an attempted cast registered a qualifying hit.

For an observation, record the target's displayed debuff before and after one action, then watch the timer without another cast. Only add another variable after that. These are proposed checks, not results of a test performed by this site.

## Does Smoke Screen make the team invincible?

Smoke Screen obscures vision and prevents aiming at targets through the smoke. Its listed type/cost/power is **Support / 10 / 0**. The wording does not say it stops every area attack, makes allies immune, heals them, or protects them after they leave its relevant area.

That distinction matters when diagnosing an apparent failure: an enemy action that did not require the blocked aim is not automatically a contradiction of the source. Do not describe the smoke as a universal shield. [Geodeback's Tunnel reference](/characters/geodeback/) is another example of protection with an explicit scope, not an equivalent mechanic.

## Which offensive actions are listed?

Roll Out is **Magic**, cost **20**, power **31**, with an aimed or lobbed mudball and area impact. ATK is a **Magic** ranged action at **0/6**. Their numerical power is not a published formula for comparing support value.

These actions give concrete candidates for observing the hit condition, but no optimal rotation, cooldown, gear set or best-team claim is established here. Check [element and role terminology](/characters/official-aniimo-types/) rather than inferring attack type from a Rock label.
