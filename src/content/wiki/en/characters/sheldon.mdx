The official description pictures Sheldon lying on its shell and gazing at the night sky, lost in a dream woven by the stars.

## Trait and puddle synergy

- **Tidal Heart:** While in water terrain or after absorbing water, Sheldon gains 20% Water damage for 10 seconds.
- **Torrent Slam:** Strikes with its shell. If water is nearby, it activates puddles in the impact area for additional damage equal to 30% Might and grants 15% Damage Amp for 20 seconds.

Both the trait and the first skill encourage Sheldon to fight around water terrain and puddles.
