## What should a compendium entry record?

A useful Aniimo entry should separate the name, location, acquisition method, and source. Version-sensitive traits and locations should also include a verification date.

This wiki separates official announcements, in-game observations, and community leads. A claim without a source is not presented as confirmed data.

## A simple verification routine

1. Check the in-game compendium or an in-game screenshot.
2. Compare the result with the official Aniimo website and update notices.
3. Cross-check related map, quest, and item pages.
4. If the evidence is incomplete, label the entry as unverified.
