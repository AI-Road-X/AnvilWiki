The official description says that Celestis love food. When searching the forest for food, the glowing stars on their heads are often mistaken for comets; they also like hoarding food to adorn their nests. The official page currently lists Basic Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Climb Lv.1**: supports basic climbing traversal.
- **Fly Lv.1**: supports basic flying traversal.
- **Grand Sorcerer**: for 5 seconds after using a skill, basic-attack hits deal an additional 6 Might of damage.
