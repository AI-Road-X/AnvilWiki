## Rage and Claw Mark mechanics

**Raging Rampage** builds Rage from combat: basic attacks and incoming damage grant 2 points each, while every 10 EP spent on skills grants 10. At 100 points, Grizbo becomes Enraged for 20 seconds, gaining 30% attack speed and skill damage plus 25% EP recovery; Rage cannot increase during this state.

Claw skills apply **Claw Mark** while Enraged. Earthquake clears marks and each stack adds 10% Might damage and a 2-second stun; Critical Hit also removes marks, with each adding 5% Might damage. Rock Smash immediately grants 100 Rage and enters Enraged.
