## What is Helion's main combat decision?

Helion's published kit asks you to choose which skill receives Solar Halo. Four basic attacks grant that state to enhance the next skill. A short sequence can therefore serve different needs: recover EP, establish a damage buff or add control. This is a conditional choice, not evidence of one best rotation.

| Basic Form field | Published value |
| --- | --- |
| Number / element / role | No.99998 / Light / DPS |
| Total | 514 |
| HP / BREAK / ATK | 100 / 64 / 116 |
| M.DEF / P.DEF / REGEN | 72 / 72 / 90 |

The trait also describes a 30% final-damage increase when the attack does **not** have an advantageous element. That wording must not be inverted into an elemental-advantage bonus. Use the [type reference](/characters/official-aniimo-types/) when labels are unclear.

## Which skill should receive Solar Halo?

Choose the enhanced effect that answers your immediate problem, then check whether the state was actually available. The source assigns different outcomes to different skills. A resource recovery value is not a damage measurement, and a control extension is not a guarantee that every enemy can be controlled.

| Action | Relevant published effect |
| --- | --- |
| Sun-Kissed | Restores 20 EP; Solar Halo adds five |
| Holy Roar | A hit grants 6% Light damage per stack, up to three, for 20s; Halo applies three stacks each time |
| Holy Storm | Its Halo enhancement adds a one-second stun to the final hit |

If the priority is EP recovery, the stated enhanced Sun-Kissed amount is **25 EP**, before any unlisted restrictions or modifiers. That arithmetic does not prove that EP above a resource cap is retained. If control is the priority, examine the last hit rather than treating the entire Holy Storm animation as a stun.

## Is Solar Halo the same as Ring of the Golden Sun?

No. The official text uses two different names. Light Bombardment lists a damage condition involving Ring of the Golden Sun, while Helios' Judgment describes evolving into Soleon and gaining that ring. Possessing Solar Halo alone is not stated to satisfy the ring requirement.

Helios' Judgment also lists a 15% self-damage increase until leaving combat. Do not copy that description into Basic Form attributes or assume the page proves a permanent collection unlock. It describes a skill outcome; it does not supply a complete acquisition or persistence guide.

## How can you compare an upgrade without mixing states?

Hold the activity, equipment and target constant, then record basic-attack count, the enhanced skill and whether the ring was present. Compare like states before changing investment. Otherwise a better result may simply come from using a different trigger rather than improving the same action.

The [single-upgrade worksheet](/tier-list/how-to-read-rankings/) gives fields for that record. For another conditional damage option, see [Infergon](/characters/infergon/); use the [role comparison](/tier-list/getting-started/) if the team needs healing or BREAK instead.
