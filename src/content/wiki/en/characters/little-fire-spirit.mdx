## What does Little Fire Spirit support?

Little Fire Spirit combines recipient-dependent healing with several differently worded Fire effects. It is not enough to label all of them “team damage”: party presence, Energy, a timed skill and nearby allies are separate conditions. Decide whether the current problem is survival or Fire support before comparing another candidate.

| Basic Form field | Published value |
| --- | --- |
| Number / element / role | No.11001 / Fire / Support |
| Total | 517 |
| HP / BREAK / ATK | 107 / 50 / 113 |
| M.DEF / P.DEF / REGEN | 88 / 60 / 99 |

For an alternative healing mechanism, compare [Melloblum](/characters/melloblum/); for element-restricted healing, see [Bulbly](/characters/bulbly/). These are different mechanics, not a performance ranking.

## Whose HP determines Treat Feed's healing?

The amount is based on **Little Fire Spirit's own HP**, while the recipient's remaining HP selects the stated tier. Those two inputs must not be swapped. The skill lists cost 30 and power zero; zero displayed power does not mean that a Support action restores no HP.

| Recipient condition | Published healing amount |
| --- | --- |
| Normal tier | 8% of the spirit's own HP |
| Below 50% HP | 12% of the spirit's own HP |
| Below 25% HP | 16% of the spirit's own HP |

For arithmetic only, suppose the spirit has 10,000 actual combat HP: the three amounts would be 800, 1,200 and 1,600 before other modifiers. This is not a measured build. Do not substitute the reference HP attribute of 107, use the recipient's maximum HP as the scaling base, or count all three tiers together.

## Which Fire effects are conditional?

Read each effect's target and activation condition separately. Reliable Fire Companion refers to party presence and Energy; Inspiration has a stated duration; the large-area skill affects nearby allies. Different labels and durations do not establish one additive multiplier or permanent coverage for every teammate.

| Source | Stated effect and condition |
| --- | --- |
| Reliable Fire Companion | Party Fire Elemental Boost 15%; 20% when Energy is above 40 |
| Inspiration | Team Fire Elemental Damage +15% for 12s |
| Keep the Fire Bolt going together! | Nearby allies' Fire Elemental Damage +25% for 30s |

“Above 40” excludes exactly 40 under the written condition. The source does not establish that the word Energy is interchangeable with every EP display. Do not add the percentages into a claimed final-damage increase without a verified stacking formula.

## How should you compare it with a Fire damage option?

Record who needs healing, the spirit's actual HP, the recipient's HP fraction and which buff was active. Change one support choice at a time with the [comparison worksheet](/tier-list/how-to-read-rankings/). [Infergon](/characters/infergon/) and [Magmarex](/characters/magmarex/) have their own Fire Debuff conditions; sharing Fire does not prove a best team or guarantee every buff interaction.
