The official description says that the scorch marks on Scorchhowl’s body are proof of victories in battle, and that it seeks strong opponents to dominate. The official page lists Basic Form, Highland Form, Thunderstorm Form, Prismana Form, and Mountain Woods Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Hustle**: enters the Hustle state and consumes stamina to increase movement speed.
- **Scorching Flames**: increases damage dealt to elementally countered targets by 25%.
