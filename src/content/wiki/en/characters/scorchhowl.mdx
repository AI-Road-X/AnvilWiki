## When do Fire Kick and Scorching Flames give their bonuses?

Fire Kick's extra effects require successfully dodging an attack; pressing the button is not the same condition. Its description gives a 120% damage result on that trigger and a 20% damage increase lasting 20 seconds. Scorching Flames separately adds 25% damage against elementally countered targets, not every opponent.

| Kick | Cost / power | Successful-dodge effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Kick | 0 / 72 | 120% damage; 20% damage increase for 20 seconds |
| Pebble Kick | 0 / 40 | 120% damage; Lockdown for 8 seconds |
| Lightning Kick | 0 / 40 | 120% damage; Paralysis for 5 seconds |

Each kick describes a brief invincible leap, not an invincible fight. The source does not specify its timing window or how different modifiers combine. “120% damage” must not become “120% extra damage.” Read the [type terminology guide](/characters/official-aniimo-types/) before interpreting element labels.

## How much EP does Burn Breaker use?

Burn Breaker lists cost 30 and power 84. At maximum charge, it consumes all remaining EP and gains 30% extra damage per additional 10 EP spent. That resource commitment differs from simply reserving the printed base cost for repeated casts.

For example, **20 extra EP** contains two complete groups of 10, corresponding to **60% extra damage in the stated bonus**. This is arithmetic on the wording, not a measured final hit. Partial-group rounding, the full damage formula and optimal timing remain unverified.
