## How do you check Surge's healing?

Surge heals the entire team from a baseline of 4% of Piopiota's maximum HP. Water within ten meters enables puddle absorption, with up to four puddles for one cast. Set up puddles with Surf and record the number absorbed when comparing heal amounts.

Start with a cast away from water. For example, 2,500 maximum HP gives a baseline of 100 healing before other effects. Then create puddles with Surf (cost 20, power 50, four puddles) and compare casts with the same missing HP and different numbers of puddles absorbed.

Surge costs 20 and lists power 72. The source describes doubling per absorbed puddle. Measure the one-puddle and multi-puddle outcomes before using a stacking formula for planning. Dark Tide has its own water check and adds stacks of BREAK damage reduction. Record the healing and mitigation casts separately.
