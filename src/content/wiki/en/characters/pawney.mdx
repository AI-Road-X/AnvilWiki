## Mobility and trait

- **Hookshot:** Extends an arm toward the center of view and pulls Pawney toward a wall or enemy on hit. Hold the skill button to aim.
- **Pursuit:** Adds 30% Damage Amp when Pawney damages a target in Controlled or Break State. The bonus depends on the target's state; it is not an unconditional always-on increase.
