## How do you separate the two slash triggers?

Land all three hits of Flowing Water Slash to trigger its extra slash. Nearby water separately empowers the slashes with additional damage. Sherro's Basic Form lists Tidal Heart at 25% Water Elemental Boost for 20 seconds when in water terrain or after absorbing water.

First check all three hits on a stationary target away from water. Then repeat near water with the same equipment. Flowing Water Slash (cost 20, power 70) grants the extra slash for landing the sequence and lists 30% Might additional damage for the empowered water blades.

Compared with Shelly's entry, Sherro has a longer Tidal Heart window (20 versus ten seconds), and its source labels the effect Water Elemental Boost. The official Basic Form page also displays other-form slam skills and unfinished numeric fields. For those variants, confirm the form and check the in-game description before comparing their damage.
