## How do Tunnel and Earth Affinity work together?

Tunnel supplies conditional ranged protection, not complete invulnerability. Earth Affinity permits skills and adds 20% BREAK specifically while Tunneling. Check the current state and available stamina before counting on that benefit; a trait tied to a state should not be included in an always-on damage estimate.

- **Tunnel:** Minespine tunnels underground and becomes immune to ranged attacks while continuously consuming stamina. Pound skills can launch it into the air.
- **Earth Affinity:** While in the Tunneling state, Minespine can cast skills and deals 20% extra BREAK.

The source does not give a stamina drain rate here, so this page cannot calculate a fixed underground duration. It also expressly allows Pound skills to launch the creature. Planning around a ranged enemy is different from assuming every incoming attack or interruption will fail.

## Does Seismic Wave deal 120 damage every second?

The source lists power 120 and describes damage each second over eight seconds. It does not define the power-to-damage formula or clarify a separate power value for each tick. Multiplying 120 by eight would introduce an assumption, not produce a verified total damage or DPS result.

For a practical comparison, separate three questions: can the action continue for its described duration, can the target remain in its effective area, and can your current resources support its cost? These are checks to perform in-game, not results of a test conducted for this page. The zero cost printed for Meteors likewise does not establish zero cooldown or unrestricted repeated use.

## How does Minespine compare with Geodeback?

Both official Basic Form pages list Rock, BREAK, Tunnel and Earth Affinity, but their numeric profiles differ. [Geodeback's reference](/characters/geodeback/) is a useful comparison because the state conditions overlap. Shared mechanics and adjacent compendium numbers alone do not prove an evolution path or a universal upgrade.
