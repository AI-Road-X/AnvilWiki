The official entry lists Berylline Causeway as Flamerion's habitat and gives it Homeland Ability 3. Its description says altered gas composition gives Flamerions different colors and that they like to sneak up and scare others. Listed forms are Basic, Highland, Forest, and Sea of Flowers. ## Trait and skills

- **Blazing Flames**: Flamerion always has 20 stacks of Fire Debuff; Trick Fire Ring applies 4 stacks and Fire Orb applies 3. The 20 stacks are part of the trait description, not 20 additional stacks on every skill use.

| Skill | Type | Cost | Power |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Trick Fire Ring | Magic | 20 | 42 |
| Phantom Barrage | Magic | 0 | 30 |
| Fire Orb | Magic | 20 | 50 |
| Phantom Flame Cannon | Magic | 0 | 100 |
| ATK | Magic | 0 | 6 |

The official skill descriptions add two useful details: **Phantom Barrage** attacks all Fire-Debuff-affected targets in range from a distance and restores EP based on their stacks (3.2 EP per stack, up to 28.8 EP); **Phantom Flame Cannon** deals area damage and applies 12 Fire Debuff stacks. Trick Fire Ring can deal up to 50% more damage to farther targets, and Fire Orb can be aimed by holding the skill. Confirm current-client behavior because game values may change with updates.
