Shrubclaw evolved from bay-dwelling Budclaw and retains its sunbathing habit, though excessive sun exposure has dehydrated and discolored the plants on its head. The official page lists Basic, Mudflat, Beach, and Bay Forms.

## Mobility, trait, and skills

- **Tunnel** continuously consumes stamina underground and grants immunity to ranged attacks.
- **Stealth** builds Momentum when skills deal damage, up to 6 stacks; max stacks grant 30% BREAK efficiency for 20 seconds.
