Blazen has Climb Lv.3 and High Jump. The official Basic Form entry lists **Electric** as its element. Dark Claw is the name of one of its skills, not evidence that Blazen is also a Dark-type Aniimo.

Looking for its listed habitat? Blitzwood is also covered in the [official region-name guide](/maps/official-region-names/); the source does not establish an exact spawn point.

## How Power Sustain works

After Blazen's skills land six critical hits, **Power Sustain** activates Overcharged for 6 seconds. It grants a shield equal to 5% of HP and reduces every skill's EP cost by 12, but **Blazen cannot gain UP during this state**. The critical-hit requirement means the benefit is conditional; the official entry does not provide a guaranteed time to activate it. Lightning Flash also enters Overcharged, according to its skill description.
