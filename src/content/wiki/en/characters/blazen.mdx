## What is Aniimo 046, and is Blazen Electric or Dark?

No.046 is **Blazen**. Basic and Mountain Woods are Electric, while Prismana lists Dark and Electric. Dark Claw appears in Basic's skill list too, so a skill name alone cannot establish the creature's element. Check the form label before transferring assumptions from another entry.

| Official form | Elements | Role / total |

| [Basic](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/046/basic-form) | Electric | BREAK / 530 |
| [Prismana](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/046/prismana-form) | Dark / Electric | BREAK / 530 |
| [Mountain Woods](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/046/mountain-woods-form) | Electric | BREAK / 530 |

All three list HP **100**, BREAK **104**, ATK **100**, M.DEF **70**, P.DEF **70** and REGEN **86**. Shared totals do not prove identical matchups or a universal best form. The [type guide](/characters/official-aniimo-types/) distinguishes role, element and attack type.

## How does Power Sustain activate Overcharged?

Power Sustain requires **six critical hits from skills**, not six button presses. It grants a shield equal to 5% HP and enters Overcharged for six seconds: skills cost 12 less EP, but UP cannot be gained. No guaranteed time to accumulate those hits is published.

For a listed **20 EP** skill, subtracting the stated reduction gives **8 EP** while the modifier applies. This does not establish free actions, negative costs or state refresh rules. The HP attribute of 100 is not enough to calculate a five-point combat shield.

Lightning Flash also explicitly enters Overcharged. Its description does not separately repeat every shield or timer detail from the trait; do not assume identical entry effects without observing them. Plan around the UP restriction, rather than treating the state as an unconditional benefit.

## Which skills explain the critical-hit condition?

Lightning Cross and Lightning Claw describe extra critical rate and multiple hits, making them relevant to checking the trait. That does not prove one use always supplies six critical hits. Target contact, actual critical results and the current state must still be observed in-game.

| Skill | Cost / power | Published behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lightning Cross | 20 / 45 | Up to three hits; additional 50% critical rate |
| Electrified Impact | 20 / 33 | Unblockable dash; a critical hit restores 5 EP |
| Dark Claw | 20 / 30 | Consecutive strikes; silence for 4 seconds |
| Lightning Claw | 20 / 20 | Three-hit combo; additional 50% critical rate |
| ATK | 0 / 6 | Close-range lightning attack |
| Lightning Flash | 0 / 135 | Leaping slam; enters Overcharged |

The cited entries classify these as Physical. Additional critical rate is not guaranteed critical hits, and an unblockable dash is not immunity to all damage. No cooldown, equipable rotation or measured DPS is supplied here.
