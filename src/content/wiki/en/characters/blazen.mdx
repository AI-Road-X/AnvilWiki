The official description says that Blazen uses its special fur to capture charged particles in the air, producing and storing electricity. The official page lists Basic Form, Prismana Form, and Mountain Woods Form.

## Mobility abilities

- **Climb Lv.3**: supports climbing-related traversal.
- **High Jump**: allows a higher jump.

## Trait: Power Sustain

After skills land 6 critical hits, Blazen enters **Overcharged**, immediately gaining a shield equal to 5% HP. The state lasts 6 seconds; all skills cost 12 less EP, but UP cannot be gained during it.
