## What is Aniimo 067?

No.067 is **Infergon**. Its official Basic Form lists Fire and the DPS role, while the Prismana page lists Fire and Wind. Both show 505 total attributes. The extra element is a confirmed form distinction, not evidence that Prismana has higher listed stats or a proven damage ranking.

## Trait and combat approach

**Power of Fire** deals 30% extra damage to targets with more than five Fire Debuff stacks. Infergon's Lava Breath and Hot Breath each apply eight stacks, giving it a direct way to meet that threshold. These are official trait and skill descriptions, not a ranking or build guarantee.

“More than five” means six or more, not five. As a simple stack-count illustration, eight stacks minus two leaves six, while consuming another two leaves four. This is not a tested attack rotation: the published descriptions do not settle debuff duration, damage resolution order or every interaction. Check the target's current stacks rather than assuming the bonus remains active throughout a combo.
