## Why didn't Appeal activate?

Appeal checks your party when Susuta enters battle. An opposite-sex member of the Susuta family activates a 30% increase to Susuta's BREAK efficiency for 15 seconds, subject to a 20-second cooldown. Check the entry event and the party member before comparing results.

Check the family and sex of the other party member first. Then check when Susuta entered and whether the 20-second cooldown had elapsed. In a clean comparison, record the buff and the effect on the same enemy's break gauge under each party setup.

Water Cannon (cost 20, power 65) creates a puddle and deals area damage. Water Dash (20, 74) knocks the target back. Use the cannon when you want to leave a puddle at the target, or the dash when moving the target helps. The official habitat is Echoback Landing and the listed pathfinding ability is Swim Lv.1.
