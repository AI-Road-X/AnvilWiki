## What is Aniimo 073?

No.073 is **Geodeback**, listed as Rock with the BREAK role and 486 total attributes. Its distinctive mechanic is Tunnel, paired with Earth Affinity. The table records the published Basic Form values; it is not a capture route or a tested comparison against every other BREAK option.

The official description says minerals Geodeback eats grow back on its body with refined energy stored inside. No habitat is listed on the official Basic Form entry, so this page does not infer one.

## Mobility and trait

- **Tunnel:** Digs underground and becomes immune to ranged attacks while continuously consuming stamina. Pound skills can launch the Aniimo into the air.
- **Earth Affinity:** While Tunneling, Geodeback can cast skills and deals 20% extra BREAK.

The source describes protection from ranged attacks specifically; it does not say the state blocks every attack type. The cover diagrams these conditions; it is an original reference graphic, not a gameplay test.

This creates two separate checks during play: whether Geodeback is actually in Tunneling state, and whether it has stamina to maintain that state. Do not carry the trait's conditional bonus into an always-on damage estimate. The source also mentions Pound skills launching it into the air, so underground does not mean immune to every form of disruption. [Tuckin's Mountain Form](/characters/tuckin/) uses a different, Hummin-based BREAK mechanic; its summon rules should not be applied to Geodeback.
