The official entry lists Sea of Flowers as Sparki's habitat and gives it Homeland Ability 2. Its description says Sparkis absorb pollen as fuel and cautions players to watch for them in Sea of Flowers at night. Listed forms are Basic, Highland, Forest, and Sea of Flowers. Check the [official region-name reference](/maps/official-region-names/) for the area's spelling.

## Trait and skills

- **Blazing Flames**: Sparki always has 20 stacks of Fire Debuff; Trick Fire Ring applies 4 stacks and Fire Orb applies 3. The 20 stacks are part of the trait description, not 20 additional stacks applied by each skill use.

| Skill | Type | Cost | Power |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Trick Fire Ring | Magic | 20 | 42 |
| Fire Orb | Magic | 20 | 50 |
| ATK | Magic | 0 | 6 |

The official entry adds that Trick Fire Ring expands and contracts around its target, dealing more damage at greater distances, up to 50% increased damage. Fire Orb can be aimed by holding the skill. Skill behavior and values may change with updates, so confirm them in the current client.
