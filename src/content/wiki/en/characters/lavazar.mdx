The official description says Lavazar crushes crystals with its claws and eats them to store heat, creating fiery red patterns on its body. Despite its small size, it can continuously breathe high-temperature flames.

## Trait and skill synergy

**Pyro Charge** is gained after spending a total amount of EP; the official page says it applies one Fire Debuff stack to nearby targets every 502 seconds for 10 seconds. The timing is reproduced as listed by the game reference. **Fiery Punch** then removes all Fire Debuff stacks from its target, with each stack adding damage equal to 5% Might.
