The official description says Bouldus was a stone ball that stayed with Baleetle, gained consciousness through repeated bumps, and swallowed Baleetle to move by its own will. Details here follow the [official Basic Form entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/058/basic-form).

## Trait and synergy

- **Rock Empowerment:** Rock Piles created by Bouldus can be destroyed by Waleetle's Stone Ball Drift, releasing a shock wave and applying Disintegration. Each stack reduces Earth Resistance by 6% and Movement Speed by 5% for 20 seconds, stacking up to 6 times.

The official page renders the resistance figure with a duplicated percent sign (“6%%”); this entry records the apparent 6% value, but the live game display should be treated as authoritative. See the [Waleetle compendium](/characters/waleetle/) for the linked skill.
