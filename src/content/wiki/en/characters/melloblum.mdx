## What role does Melloblum fill?

The official compendium classifies Melloblum as **Support**. Its published effects connect teammates' basic attacks, lower EP costs, critical-hit-triggered healing and Grass damage support. That combination gives specific reasons to consider it for a team; the label alone does not establish a best-in-slot build or a tested tier.

## How does the trait reduce EP costs?

- **Phantom Opening** grants 1 Rose Mark stack when a teammate deals basic attack damage, up to 10 stacks. Each stack reduces Melloblum's EP cost by 3.

| Trait stacks | Listed EP reduction, by arithmetic |
| ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 3 |
| 5 | 15 |
| 10 | 30 |

This table multiplies the stated stack count by three. It does not prove that a skill becomes free, returns EP, or preserves its stacks after use: the cited description does not explain a minimum cost or consumption rule. The useful distinction is that teammates' **basic attack damage**, rather than any unspecified team action, is the documented source of these stacks.

## Which skills heal, cleanse or support Grass damage?

| Skill | Type | Cost | Power |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Thorny Blessing | Magic | 30 | 25 |
| Healing Rose | Magic | 20 | 40 |
| Sparkling Rose | Magic | 20 | 40 |
| Blasting Roses | Magic | 20 | 40 |
| Rose Garden | Support | 0 | 0 |
| ATK | Magic | 0 | 6 |

The table records the displayed type, cost and power. Power is not a measured final-damage or healing result, and the shared skill display does not establish which moves can be equipped together on one form.

**Healing Rose** applies a target mark that blooms after the target receives three critical hits. The resulting heal affects nearby allies for 5% of Melloblum's HP. If the marked enemy is not taking critical hits, the described healing trigger has not been satisfied. Allies' position matters too: the wording says nearby allies, not every party member anywhere on the map.

Do not combine that target mark with the trait's EP stack counter merely because both use the Rose Mark name. The trait describes a resource gained from teammates' basic attacks; the healing skill describes a marked target and a critical-hit trigger. The official page does not explicitly say one counter pays for, replaces or consumes the other.

**Sparkling Rose** provides a different kind of support: it removes the team's negative effects, then launches a tracking Light attack. Its listed additional critical rate is 15%. **Blasting Roses** instead lists a 35% extra critical rate for its own attack. Neither description says it grants that critical-rate bonus to the entire party.

**Thorny Blessing** adds a damage effect when Grass critical damage is dealt, with at most one activation every 0.5 seconds during its 15-second duration. **Rose Garden** creates an area that improves allies' Grass critical damage for 30 seconds. Its public description prints a duplicated percent sign, so this guide does not convert that formatting issue into a confirmed numeric bonus.

## Is REGEN 78 a healing percentage?

No percentage or healing formula is provided beside the REGEN attribute. Reading 78 as “78% healing” or as a guaranteed regeneration rate would add a rule the source does not state. Use the explicit 5% in Healing Rose's description for that skill's stated recovery, and keep it separate from the compendium attribute until a formula is documented.
