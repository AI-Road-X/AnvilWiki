## Where should you end Hydro Roll?

Water terrain or absorbing water activates Shelly's Tidal Heart for ten seconds. Hydro Roll leaves a puddle at the end and can restore stamina at most once every three seconds. For Torrent Slam, place the target where the attack area overlaps a puddle.

End near the intended fight area so you can aim Torrent Slam (cost 20, power 47) to overlap the puddle with the hit area. The source gives 30% of original Might for the puddle damage and lists 15% Damage Amp for 20 seconds. Check the puddle position if the extra effect is missing.

Tidal Heart gives 20% Water damage for ten seconds under its water condition. Record that buff separately from Torrent Slam's Damage Amp. Hydro Roll (20, 83) still drains stamina while rolling. Its listed recovery interval helps you plan a short roll and leave a reserve for repositioning.
