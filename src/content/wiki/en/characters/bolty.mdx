## How Power Sustain works

After Bolty's skills land six critical hits, **Power Sustain** activates Overcharged for 6 seconds. It immediately grants a shield equal to 5% of HP and reduces every skill's EP cost by 12, but **Bolty cannot gain UP during this state**. Activation depends on landing skill critical hits; the official entry does not promise a fixed activation time.
