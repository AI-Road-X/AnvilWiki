## How do you spend the Pinecone Mark?

Hit the same target with Pinecone Bomb before landing Heavy Tail Strike. The second hit spends the Pinecone Mark for extra damage, a launch and a stun. Raging Fire separately changes the next basic attack within ten seconds of using a skill.

Pinecone Bomb (cost 15, power 38) marks enemies struck by the explosion or fragments. Heavy Tail Strike (20, 40) consumes the mark on a successful hit. The marked hit gains 50% damage, a launch and a 2.5-second stun according to the Basic Form entry.

Against two enemies, watch which one received the icon before committing the punch. If the mark disappears after the hit, apply a fresh one for your next marked strike. Raging Fire gives a separate ten-second window in which the next basic attack jumps to its finisher, with Might rising from 4 to 25.
