## Why does Fire Debuff order matter for Magmarex?

Magmarex's actions do not all use Fire Debuff in the same way. One consumes it, another checks a threshold and another scales with the existing count. Your next choice should follow the result you need. A blanket “press every skill” sequence can remove a condition before the next action checks it.

## Which actions consume, check or scale with stacks?

Choose the action by its condition rather than its displayed power alone. Lava Punch removes Fire Debuff; Magma Surge requires more than five stacks for its stun; Burning Sun lists per-stack scaling. These are separate effects, and neither the table nor the source establishes a tested damage-optimal order.

| Action | Listed cost / power | Stack interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lava Punch | 20 / 64 | Removes all stacks; additional 5% Might damage per stack |
| Magma Surge | 20 / 30 | More than five stacks enables a 0.4s stun |
| Burning Sun | 0 / 140 | Damage increases by 2% per stack on the target |
| Lava Breath | 0 / 35 | Applies Fire Debuff while dealing damage over time |

## What happens in a six-stack example?

At six stacks, the written “more than five” condition is satisfied if the stacks are still present when checked. Using the consuming action first changes that situation. This makes the count at the moment of each action more useful than the count you remember from the start of a sequence.

For a threshold test, record the count before Magma Surge and whether the stun appears. For a consumption test, record the count before and after Lava Punch. For scaling, compare the same target and conditions while recording the count. Do not interpret a stun failure as proof that the threshold wording is wrong: control resistance remains another question.

[Infergon](/characters/infergon/) also has a strict Fire Debuff threshold. Sharing an element does not make the two kits interchangeable. Use the [team comparison worksheet](/tier-list/how-to-read-rankings/) to change one candidate at a time.

## Which official details remain uncertain?

The Pyro Charge description has a missing cumulative-EP number and merged-looking timing text, including “502s.” There is no reliable basis here for silently replacing it with a guessed EP threshold or interval. The page also lists Dark Energy Surge alongside other skills; that display alone does not prove every listed variant can be equipped together in Basic Form.
