## Mobility and Rage trait

- **Head Hammer:** Use the mobility skill to launch a shock wave.
- **Raging Rampage:** Cubbo gains Rage in battle. Basic attacks and taking damage each grant 2 points; every 10 EP spent on skills grants 10. At 100 points, it becomes Enraged for 20 seconds, increasing attack speed and skill damage by 30%, increasing EP recovery by 25%, and preventing further Rage gain during the state.

The listed Rage sources mean the state can build through normal combat actions; its duration and bonuses above follow the official description.
