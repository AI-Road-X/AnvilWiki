The official description says Helgon's hard armor makes it resemble a tall statue guarding ancient treasure when it stands still. Its official Mountain Form entry says it evolves from mountain-dwelling Helmwhelp; see the [Helmwhelp compendium](/characters/helmwhelp/). Skill values below follow the [official Basic Form entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/066/basic-form).

## Mobility and trait

- **Bilateral:** Enters Bilateral mode to see a different perspective.
- **Counter Strike:** After being attacked, increases BREAK efficiency by 25% for 10 seconds.
- **Wind Counter:** Enters a defensive stance, gains 30% damage reduction, and counterattacks. If attacked at the moment the skill is cast, a successful hit also increases BREAK efficiency by 20% for 10 seconds and stuns the target for 1.5 seconds.
