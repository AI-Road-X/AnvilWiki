## What is Aniimo 022, and which form is described here?

No.022 is **Tuckin**. This entry records the official **Mountain Form**, which lists Rock and Grass with the BREAK role. The official page also links to Basic Form; do not apply Mountain Form's element labels to every form without checking the selected page. Changes in soil composition give Tuckin orange-red, thicker, and tougher skin after it absorbs minerals from the earth.

## Trait and skills

- **Prank Master** summons one Hummin on entering battle, then another every 10 seconds up to three. Its HP-scaling clause refers to each 21,000 max HP, but the published percentage is blank. We therefore cannot calculate the BREAK increase or recommend an HP target from that sentence.

The table's HP attribute of 106 is not a stated in-combat maximum HP of 106. Substituting that attribute directly into the 21,000-max-HP clause would mix two different quantities. Likewise, the summon cap is not evidence of an unlimited army or a permanent three-Hummin opening. The original cover diagram illustrates the summon count if units persist; it is not a tested timing sequence and does not fill in the missing multiplier.

## What do the Hummin skills do?

Draw Nourishment supports Tuckin and its Hummin units; the published healing targets do not establish a party-wide heal for unrelated teammates. Haunting Choir brings Hummin to the target for an area attack with a one-second stun. Breaking Roar restores the Hummin count before the joint attack. Confirm current skill text and available units before planning a sequence.

For the linked creature's own reference, see [Hummin](/characters/hummin/). To compare a different BREAK mechanic, read [Geodeback's Tunnel conditions](/characters/geodeback/). Neither that comparison nor a shared role establishes a best-team ranking.
