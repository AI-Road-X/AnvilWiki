## Which differences matter when choosing Popota?

Popota and Susuta share the listed Appeal, Water Cannon and Water Dash conditions. Popota has higher listed HP, BREAK and ATK, while Susuta has higher magical defense. Check the party requirement when planning a break window and compare the stat relevant to your encounter.

The Basic Form comparison is 92 versus 81 HP, 80 versus 72 BREAK, and 96 versus 61 ATK. Susuta has 80 M.DEF against Popota's 68. Compare the same form at comparable levels if you want to see whether those differences change a particular encounter.

Both entries describe Appeal as an entry trigger with an opposite-sex Susuta-family party member. The 30% BREAK efficiency bonus lasts 15 seconds and has a 20-second cooldown. Both Water Cannons create a puddle, and both Water Dashes knock back the target. Echoback Landing and Swim Lv.1 are listed for Popota.
