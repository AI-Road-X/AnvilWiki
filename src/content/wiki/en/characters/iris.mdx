The official data says Iris has an extremely light body that is often lifted by wind; it descends slowly in a spiral using the resistance of its petal skirt, or simply lets the wind carry it. The official page lists Basic, Highland, Forest, Prairie, Prismana, Plateau, and Mountain Woods Forms.

## Mobility and trait

- **Blooming**: flowers bloom wherever Iris dances.
- **Disco Aniimo**: while on grass, Damage Amp increases by 30%. After entering battle, each skill hit grants 1 Dance Power; hitting multiple targets grants no extra Dance Power. At 24 Dance Power, the next skill costs no EP.
