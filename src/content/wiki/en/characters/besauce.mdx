## Energy support trait

**Power Surge** restores 4 EP when Besauce casts a skill costing less than 20 EP. The official kit also includes **Charged Geyser**, which restores 15 Energy immediately and 0.8 Energy per second for 12 seconds. These are separate resources and effects as named in the official entry.
