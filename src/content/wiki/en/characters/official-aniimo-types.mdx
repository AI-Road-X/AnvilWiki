## Why start with types instead of a full character list?

Official pages currently expose several Aniimo labels but do not provide one complete roster with every stat, location, and acquisition method. This page records terms with clear official sources instead of turning community guesses into compendium data.

## Prismana Form Aniimo

The official launch material says that eligible Junior Wayfarers can choose a rare Prismana Form Aniimo. Prismana is an official form or appearance label in that material; the current selection list and event conditions should be checked in the live in-game event panel.

## Umbral Aniimo

The developer letter says that Wild Surges and the open world can provide encounters with Umbral Aniimo, and describes them as having Perfect Potential and distinctive coloration. This page records those public details without inferring a complete stat or drop table.

## Perfect Potential

Perfect Potential is an official term used to describe Aniimo potential. The developer letter also says that an Aniimo caught with a Sparkling Cube has guaranteed Perfect Potential. Check the current in-game explanation before treating that as a complete statement about training effects or caps.

## Alpha and Omega

The official probability page includes capture notes for Common Alpha and Common Omega. It says that Common Alpha has no ordinary capture chance while alive and a separate base chance after defeat, while Common Omega is marked as uncapturable. These are capture-system notes and should not be generalized to every Alpha or Omega template.
