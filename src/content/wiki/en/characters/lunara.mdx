## How does Silver Moon Mark work?

The **Lunar Power** trait has two stated effects. Attacks without elemental advantage gain a 30% final-damage increase. Separately, casting a skill or Ultimate grants four Silver Moon Mark stacks; each basic-attack hit consumes one stack for extra damage and a small HP recovery.

| Trigger or effect | Published rule | Practical interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill or Ultimate cast | Grants four stacks | Basic attacks have a role after casting; the kit is not only a chain of skills. |
| Basic-attack hit | Consumes one stack; 100% extra damage | A hit is required. The source does not promise a proc for a missed attack. |
| Recovery on that hit | 0.5% HP | This is personal recovery, not evidence of a team-wide heal. |

Four qualifying hits would produce four listed recovery triggers: arithmetically, four times 0.5% is 2% of the HP basis used by the mechanic. That is a reading of the stated rule, not an in-game healing test. The source does not define the HP basis, stack-refresh behavior or an overall stack cap.

The 30% condition also does not prove that a bad elemental matchup is optimal. Enemy resistance, base damage and other modifiers are missing from that comparison. Use the condition to understand the trait rather than to claim a universal best matchup.

Fox Mark and Silver Moon Mark serve different purposes. Lumin Bolts applies a target debuff with a listed 5% Light Resistance reduction, a three-stack limit and a ten-second duration. Silver Moon Mark is the trait resource spent by basic attacks. The matching word “Mark” does not make them interchangeable.

Because Lumin Bolts favors different targets, three missiles do not guarantee three debuff stacks on one enemy. Similarly, Moon Impact's source mentions a 10% additional effect without clearly defining its calculation basis. An exact damage calculator would need those rules before using either value as a multiplier.

## What changes when Lunara becomes Fennelun?

Selene's Judgment explicitly says that Lunara changes into **Fennelun**, gains a 15% damage increase until combat ends, and receives four mark stacks. The available wiki page still presents one Basic Form tab. A combat transformation therefore should not be presented as a separately verified capture or permanent evolution method.

A sensible kit-reading sequence is to check a target's Fox Mark, use the relevant attack or EP-recovery option, and account for the basic attacks that spend Silver Moon Mark. This describes how the published effects connect. It is not a tested rotation, a fixed skill loadout or a promise of maximum damage. Cooldowns, available slots and current client behavior still determine a usable rotation.
