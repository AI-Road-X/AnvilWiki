The official description says Glacy evolved from Sea of Flowers-dwelling Pranky and has a dreamy pink body; when running, the membranes on its back undulate like endless rolling pink waves. The official page lists Basic Form, Sea of Flowers Form, Snowfield Form, and Prismana Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Swim Lv.2**: supports higher-level water traversal.
- **Water Spirit**: while in water terrain, reduces the EP cost of all skills by 16%.
