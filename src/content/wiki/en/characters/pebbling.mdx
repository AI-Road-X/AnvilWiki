Pebbling is described as a natural geologist that eats many kinds of minerals; what it eats determines how it grows.

## Mobility and trait

- **Tunnel:** Digs underground and becomes immune to ranged attacks while continuously consuming stamina. Pound skills can launch Aniimo into the air.
- **Earth Affinity:** While Tunneling, Pebbling can cast skills and deals 20% extra BREAK.

This form's official kit emphasizes staying underground and applying BREAK. The immunity is specifically described for ranged attacks; the source does not claim immunity to every attack type.
