The official description says that on a tall reef, Cornet try to make their voices travel farther, as if an echo might come from the other side of the sea. The official page lists Basic Form, Beach Form, Prismana Form, and Highland Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Fly Lv.3**: enters Flying state to evade ground attacks, but takes more damage while airborne.
- **Air Superiority**: increases critical rate by 20% while flying.
