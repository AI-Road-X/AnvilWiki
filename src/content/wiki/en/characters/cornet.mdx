## Mobility and trait

- **Fly Lv.3 / Take Off**: Take Off enters Flying state to evade ground attacks, but Cornet takes more damage while airborne.
- **Air Superiority**: increases critical rate by 20% while flying.

The trade-off is therefore explicit: flying avoids ground attacks and activates a critical-rate bonus, but it also increases incoming damage while airborne. The official pages do not provide enough information here to calculate a net damage or survival advantage.
