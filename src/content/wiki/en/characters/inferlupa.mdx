The official description says that Inferlupa is a Flameruff that challenged the strong and lost, absorbing the souls of fallen companions and becoming driven by revenge.

## Mobility and trait

- **Hustle**: enters the Hustle state and consumes stamina to increase movement speed.
- **Full Energy**: after 8 basic attacks in battle, Inferlupa enters Full Energy and enhances its next skill; damage from that skill is treated as basic-attack damage.
