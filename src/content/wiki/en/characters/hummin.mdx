## Which Hummin form does No.020 describe here?

No.020 is **Hummin**, and this page covers the official **Mountain Form**. Its published labels are Grass and BREAK. The source also offers a Basic Form link, but a form selector is not evidence that all forms share these values. Match the selected form before comparing stats, habitats or skills.

The current official English habitat label is **Berylline Causeway**; older wording on this page used Beryl Boardwalk. These are regional labels, not precise spawn points. For route planning, see the [official-region reference](/maps/official-region-names/) and avoid treating a region name as a confirmed coordinate or guaranteed encounter.

## What does Energized scale with?

The current official trait name is **Energized**. Its description relates a 2% BREAK-damage increase to each 1,000 maximum HP. The profile's HP attribute of 94 is a different quantity from a developed creature's in-combat maximum HP. Read the actual maximum-HP value rather than substituting 94 into that clause.

| Quantity | How to read it | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HP attribute: 94 | One listed profile attribute | The creature's final combat HP |
| Maximum HP | The quantity named by Energized | A published optimum build target |
| BREAK attribute: 80 | Another listed profile attribute | A direct percentage or a measured damage result |

The cited sentence does not explain rounding, caps or every interaction. A build calculator would need those rules in addition to the trait text. That is why this reference does not manufacture an ideal HP threshold or declare that every HP item is better than every alternative.

## Which skill names and effects are current?

The current page names the mobility entry **Dig In** and describes being rooted in soil. This does not establish ranged immunity: [Geodeback's Tunnel](/characters/geodeback/) is a different named ability. The skill table below retains the official names, while the effect notes distinguish what the published text actually says.

| Skill | Type | Cost | Power | Published effect, summarized |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Gravel Tackle | Physical | 10 | 39 | A body ram described as Earth damage. |
| Euphoric Sonic Blast | Magic | 0 | 30 | Frontal-cone attack; the description also says it restores 10 energy. |
| Plant Charge | Physical | 10 | 39 | A body-charge attack. |
| ATK | Physical | 0 | 6 | Close-range Grass attack marked BREAK. |

The energy sentence does not identify a team-wide energy-restoration effect. Likewise, a skill using Earth damage does not change the form's listed Grass element. Keep the creature's labels, skill types and resource effects separate when comparing options.

## Is this profile the same as Tuckin's summoned Hummin?

[Tuckin's Mountain Form](/characters/tuckin/) names Hummin in its summon and support descriptions, but those descriptions belong to Tuckin's own kit. The No.020 profile does not explain whether a summoned unit inherits a captured Hummin's equipment, maximum HP or every trait. Do not transfer build numbers between them without evidence.

Use this page for Hummin's published profile, Tuckin's page for its summon timing and cap, and the [verification notes](/characters/verification-notes/) to distinguish sourced rules from tests still needed. Neither page establishes a universal team ranking.
