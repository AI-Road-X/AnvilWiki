## What its support skills actually cover

- **Healing Impact** heals allies *within range* at 2% HP per second while channeled. After 3 seconds of continuous casting, it removes negative status effects from Luminelle itself; the entry does not say it cleanses the whole team.
- **Lightning Discharge** heals *Lightning-type teammates in range* at 2% HP per second. That narrower target condition matters if your team contains other elements.
- **Lightning Surge**, the current official skill name, grants Lightning-type teammates 27% more basic-attack damage and 16% more BREAK efficiency for 20 seconds. Earlier versions of this page used the label Spotlight Moment.
- **Water Rush** appears in both checked form pages and specifies healing for Water-element allies in range. Check the available skill in your current loadout and the recipient's element.
- **Arc Surge** gives teammates who receive Luminelle's skill buff an additional 12% Lightning boost for 20 seconds.

When comparing support options, first check your active form, teammates' elements, whether they stay in the skill's range, and whether channeling can be maintained. ## How do you choose a heal for a mixed team?

Choose the intended recipient first, then compare the skill's target rule with that teammate's element. Healing Impact, Lightning Discharge and Water Rush have different wording. During a test encounter, watch the intended ally's HP while changing one condition at a time, such as range, active skill or recipient element.

1. Read the form and equipped skill on the current client.
2. Move the intended recipient into the skill's range.
3. Hold the required channel and watch the recipient's HP change.
4. For the status-removal effect, check Luminelle's own status after continuous channeling.
5. Repeat with the same recipient before changing another condition.

Healing Impact's three-second status removal applies to **self** in the checked wording. Keep that target distinct from the allies receiving its healing. ## What should you check before investing in a Lightning team?

Identify which party member needs the buff and which attack they will use during its window. Then observe whether the intended recipient actually receives the skill buff that triggers Arc Surge. This separates a teammate being present in the party from a teammate receiving the effect during your chosen sequence.
