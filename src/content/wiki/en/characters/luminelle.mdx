The official entries show the same compendium number across forms. Do not treat the Rainstorm Form's Water element as proof that the Basic Form is Water-type. A habitat name also does not identify an exact spawn point.

## What its support skills actually cover

- **Healing Impact** heals allies *within range* at 2% HP per second while channeled. After 3 seconds of continuous casting, it removes negative status effects from Luminelle itself; the entry does not say it cleanses the whole team.
- **Lightning Discharge** heals *Lightning-type teammates in range* at 2% HP per second. That narrower target condition matters if your team contains other elements.
- **Spotlight Moment** grants Lightning-type teammates 27% more basic-attack damage and 16% more BREAK efficiency for 20 seconds, according to the official skill text. This is a team buff, not a published overall damage ranking.
- **Water Rush** appears in both cited form pages and specifies healing for Water-element allies in range. The target condition does not by itself change the Basic Form's listed Electric element. The Rainstorm Form, separately, is listed as Electric/Water.

When comparing support options, first check your active form, teammates' elements, whether they stay in the skill's range, and whether channeling can be maintained.
