This entry cites the official **Mountain Woods Form** page, so Beast Fang Ridge is the habitat confirmed here. Official pages for different forms may show different habitats and descriptions; do not treat one form's details as the shared distribution for every form. The Mountain Woods description highlights the golden tips of its dark fur as a sign of confidence and pride as a pack leader. The official index lists Basic, Highland, and Mountain Woods forms.

## Mobility and trait

- **Hustle**: enters the Hustle state and consumes stamina to increase movement speed.
- **Scorching Flames**: increases damage dealt to elementally countered targets by 25%.
