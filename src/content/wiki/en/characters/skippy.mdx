The official description says cold-region Skippy do not feel the snow's cold at all; instead, they enjoy the refreshing feeling of the frozen lotus-leaf hat on their heads. The official page lists Basic Form, Sea of Flowers Form, and Snowfield Form.

## Mobility and trait

- **Swim Lv.1**: supports basic water traversal.
- **Water Spirit**: while in water terrain, reduces the EP cost of all skills by 16%.
