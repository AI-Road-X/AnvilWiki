## How Overloaded Engine works

Baleetle's **Overloaded Engine** builds Overload Energy while moving and using skills. Once the gauge is full, the next skill deals **40% bonus damage**. This makes action order relevant: build the gauge through normal movement and skill use, then spend the powered-up skill on a useful target. The official description does not specify a universal rotation or exact gauge threshold, so check the in-game skill text rather than assuming a fixed number of actions.

The listed mobility is **Hustle**, which increases movement speed while consuming stamina. Treat it as an exploration and repositioning tool with a stamina cost, not as a permanent speed bonus.
