## Mobility and trait

- **Scurry:** Rushes forward a short distance.
- **Thunderbond:** An attack hit grants 1 stack. At 30 stacks, Fenmane enters Thunderwing Stance when using its next skill or Ultimate; the stance lasts 10 seconds.
