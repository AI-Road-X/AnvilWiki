Its feathers secrete oil that forms bubbles through friction. The official description says Erlath uses dense, soft foam to protect the Fahloos riding on its back. For another bubble-focused Water Aniimo, see [Fahloo](/characters/fahloo/).

## Trait and support role

- **Bubble:** Every 12 seconds Erlath enters Bathing; its next skill creates a puddle.
- **Floaty Bubble:** Scrubs itself to create a large bubble around itself.

The Basic Form kit combines team EP recovery, puddle interaction, crowd control, and a temporary Water-damage buff.
