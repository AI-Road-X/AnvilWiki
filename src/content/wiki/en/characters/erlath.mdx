Its feathers secrete oil that forms bubbles through friction. The official description says Erlath uses dense, soft foam to protect the Fahloos riding on its back. For another bubble-focused Water Aniimo, see [Fahloo](/characters/fahloo/).

## Trait and support role

- **Bubble:** Every 12 seconds Erlath enters Bathing; its next skill creates a puddle.
- **Floaty Bubble:** Scrubs itself to create a large bubble around itself.

The Basic Form kit combines team EP recovery, puddle interaction, crowd control, and a temporary Water-damage buff.

## How do you build bubbles for EP recovery?

Start with the field, move the intended Water skill users into it, and then check the bubble count before recasting. The trigger depends on a teammate's Water skill being cast inside the area. A creature's Water element alone does not show that the required cast has happened. The field creates a short window for coordinating your available skills.

1. Identify which teammate can actually cast a Water skill in your current loadout.
2. Place the Bubbles field where that teammate can remain while acting.
3. Watch the counter or visual effect as skills are used, then reactivate to detonate.
4. Check the EP change before starting another setup. Moving away or recasting early can make your result differ from the full setup.

Six bubbles at the listed 4 EP each produce a **24 EP bubble subtotal**, calculated from the official values. The puddle bonus is described separately. Check its application in the current client before budgeting a larger total for a rotation.

## When should you choose Erlath for a Water team?

Use this kit comparison when your immediate problem is keeping Water skills available. If the problem is survival, compare what your team needs to the conditions in [Piopiota's healing guide](/characters/piopiota/) and [Panpanta's shield guide](/characters/panpanta/). Their recovery, shielding and positioning checks answer different practical questions.

Before investing, take the same team into a repeatable encounter and record the EP before the field, the Water casts made inside it, the bubble count at detonation and the EP afterward. Change one condition at a time. This lets you test whether your actual setup can use the published mechanic consistently.
