## What should you do first in Aniimo?

For a smooth start, follow the opening tutorial and story prompts before roaming far from the current objective. Learn the controls at your own pace, check the map when you need orientation, and treat nearby discoveries as optional detours. Early on, focus on understanding the menus and your next objective rather than trying to collect or upgrade everything at once.

The official site describes Idyll as an exploration-focused world with quests, Aniimo, and activities. Details can change between updates, so use the instructions shown in your current game client as the authority; this guide is a route-planning aid, not a substitute for the latest in-game prompts. See the [official launch overview](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100147) for current cross-platform account information.

## How can you make a useful first-session plan?

Use this short loop whenever you start playing:

1. **Check the active objective.** Read the quest or activity text and note the place or item it names.
2. **Orient yourself.** Open the in-game map and identify the objective area before setting off. The map article can help you compare locations: [exploration maps](/maps/).
3. **Take one nearby detour.** If an Aniimo or resource catches your eye, investigate it, but return to the objective if the detour becomes unclear.
4. **Review what you found.** Look up unfamiliar creatures in the [Aniimo character index](/characters/) and items in the [item directory](/items/).
5. **Re-check the objective.** After a story step or discovery, see whether the game has introduced a new task or menu feature.

This keeps exploration open-ended without leaving you wondering what to do next. If the game presents a specific tutorial instruction, follow that over any general advice here.

## What should you prioritize before spending resources?

First learn which items are easy to replace and which the game marks as limited, event-based, or especially valuable. Read an item's description and check the relevant [item reference](/items/) before committing materials to an upgrade or one-time choice. Avoid spending something simply because a menu offers it; wait until you understand what the upgrade changes and whether it supports your current goal.

You do not need a perfect team or a complete collection to progress through the opening. Choose a small goal—such as learning one new Aniimo, finishing the current quest, or exploring one marked area—and let that goal guide any optional spending. Capture and item details can be version-sensitive, so confirm the current rules in-game and use the [capture-tool reference](/items/official-catch-tools/) for additional context.

## Where should you go when you are stuck?

Read the exact objective text again, check the map for its named location, and look for any new prompt or interaction marker nearby. If an objective mentions a creature, area, or item, the [character index](/characters/), [maps](/maps/), and [item directory](/items/) are the quickest reference points. For story steps and task terminology, browse the [quest guides](/quests/).

If the game itself behaves unexpectedly, such as an objective not updating, restart from the current in-game instructions and official announcements rather than relying on old beta-era keybinds or mechanics. The official [September 2026 update notice](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100145) is a better source for dated changes than older test guides.

## What is a good next goal after the tutorial?

Pick one of these and continue at your own pace: advance the next story objective, fill in a nearby section of the Aniimo index, or explore a location you have not visited. Check the map and relevant reference page first, then return to the quest list when you want a clear direction. For reward codes, see the [current codes page](/codes/); always verify that a code is still active before trying it.
