## Are the launch rewards still claimable?

The [official September 16 launch announcement](https://www.aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100115) described the rewards below. It does **not** establish that every launch event is still open today. Check the in-game event panel, mailbox and newer official notices for the current claim window and account eligibility. This article is a record of the announced launch offers, not an active-code list.

| Reward path | Who or what was required in the announcement | What to check now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prismana Form Aniimo | Reach Grade I Wayfarer, then choose from the offered options | Current event panel and whether you already claimed a choice |
| Aniilog accessories | Catch and collect Aniimo to reach collection milestones | Aniilog progress and current reward rules |
| Catching items | Participate in launch-version activities and events | Whether the relevant event is still available |
| Pre-registration milestones | Qualified launch accounts; all four global tiers were reached | Mailbox and account used for pre-registration |
| Susuta egg | Official-site pre-registration before launch | The matching account and mailbox |
| Login egg and outfits | Meet the relevant cumulative-login or story/activity conditions | Current event terms and claim deadlines |

## Choosing a Prismana Form Aniimo

The announcement said players reaching **Grade I Wayfarer** could choose a Prismana Form Aniimo. Confirm that the choice remains available in your current event panel before planning around it; do not rely on an old community screenshot.

## Catching Aniimo and Aniilog rewards

The official page also explains that catching and collecting Aniimo unlocks the Aniilog, with Aniimo accessories available through that collection path. It is useful to record the Aniimo name, source, and event condition before planning a collection route.

## Launch-event catching items

The launch announcement named these catching-related items as possible event rewards:

- Sparkling Cube
- Aniipod Ultra
- Tumbler

This article records the names shown by the official page and does not infer quantities or drop conditions. Check the live event panel for the current details.

## Login, egg, and milestone rewards

The announcement also listed these reward paths:

- a Susuta egg connected to pre-registration and login activities;
- a Random Perfect Aniimo Egg tied to cumulative login days;
- the Sunlit Meadow limited outfit;
- the Companion Promise limited avatar frame;
- pre-registration milestone rewards including Aniipod Ultra ×2 and Glimmer ×200, as well as the outfit and avatar frame above.

If a reward does not arrive immediately, check the in-game mailbox, the event panel, and the account used for the activity. Avoid unofficial redemption pages.
