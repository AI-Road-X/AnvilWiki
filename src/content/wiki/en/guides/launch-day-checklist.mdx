## A sensible first-day order

1. Finish the tutorial and learn movement, combat, and map markers.
2. Advance the main story to unlock important systems.
3. Record Aniimo you have encountered before planning a collection route.
4. Keep rare and unknown-purpose items until their use is confirmed.
5. Unlock the mailbox before processing code and event rewards.

Do not spend all rare resources based on an unverified tier list. Check the [items guide](/items/getting-started/) and [compendium verification notes](/characters/verification-notes/) first.

## What is worth recording?

Keep the quest name, region, key items, Aniimo encounters, and game version. This makes it easier to identify outdated advice after an update.
