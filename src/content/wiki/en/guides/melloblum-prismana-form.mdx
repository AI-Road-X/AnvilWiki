## What changes in Melloblum's Prismana form?

The official [Prismana entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/035/prismana-form) adds **holy** beside **Grass**. The [Basic entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/035/basic-form) lists Grass alone. Both identify the creature as No.035 and classify it as Support. If you searched for “Melloblum Prismana,” this element difference is the main directly documented answer.

| Comparison | Basic Form | Prismana Form |

| Listed elements | Grass | holy, Grass |
| Official role | Support | Support |
| Attribute total | 517 | 517 |
| HP / BREAK / ATK | 95 / 91 / 113 | 95 / 91 / 113 |
| M.DEF / P.DEF / REGEN | 70 / 70 / 78 | 70 / 70 / 78 |
| Named habitat on the checked page | Rosetower Woods | No habitat section shown |
| Trait heading | Phantom Opening | Phantom Opening |

The comparison describes the public pages checked on October 20, 2026. Equal listed numbers do not establish that two player-owned creatures have identical final builds: level, investment, equipment and other live-client factors are not represented by this table. Conversely, the extra element is not evidence that every statistic or every skill has been upgraded.

## Is Prismana automatically the stronger form?

The available evidence does not support that ranking. Prismana has a different element listing, but the displayed attributes and role remain the same. A meaningful strength comparison would need the same activity, comparable investment, actual available skills and recorded results. None of those results is supplied by the form label itself.

For a team that wants recovery, the important question is whether the healing skill and its activation conditions fit the fight. For Grass critical support, the damage type and positioning are more relevant than the word Prismana alone. The [Melloblum skill guide](/characters/melloblum/) explains those conditions without assigning an unsupported tier.

## Do the form pages prove separate skill loadouts?

Both pages show the same named trait and the same skill headings, including Healing Rose and Sparkling Rose. That makes them useful references for skill wording, but it does not prove that all listed moves fit into one loadout or that each is available under identical unlock conditions.

Keep three labels separate when checking a build: the creature's form, the creature's element list and a skill's own effect or element. Sparkling Rose describes a Light attack, while several other effects specifically support Grass damage. One extra creature element cannot be used as a rule that converts every move to that element.

## Where can you find Prismana Melloblum?

The checked Prismana page does not publish a habitat section. Basic Form lists Rosetower Woods, but copying that label into a claimed Prismana spawn route would go beyond the evidence. Neither page supplies a verified spawn coordinate, capture probability, required item or transformation procedure for obtaining this form.

Use the current in-game compendium or an official acquisition notice if one is available to your account. Regional references such as the [official region-name guide](/maps/official-region-names/) can explain a place name; they cannot turn an absent form-specific location into a guaranteed encounter.

## What should you compare before investing resources?

1. Check which form your client actually names and which elements it displays.
2. Read the available skill slots and unlock conditions, rather than treating the wiki's full skill display as a ready-made loadout.
3. Identify your concrete need: a critical-hit-triggered heal, a cleanse or Grass support. Compare that need with the published trigger and range.
4. Keep any proposed acquisition route separate from the form comparison until it has its own dated evidence.

This checklist follows from the differences between the source fields; it is not a record of a gameplay test. The same care applies to [Cornet's four forms](/characters/cornet/), whose element and habitat labels also differ between form pages. A form comparison helps answer a narrower question than a global tier list and should not be presented as one.
