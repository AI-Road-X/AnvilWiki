## Official rollout dates

In the official developer letter, the Aniimo team said the game launched worldwide on **September 16, 2026** for PC, console, and Xbox Cloud Gaming. The mobile version followed on **September 23, 2026**. Store availability, maintenance, and local display times can still vary by region.

## Start with the official download path

Use this order:

1. Open the [official Aniimo website](https://www.aniimo.com/);
2. Open its download or platform page;
3. Choose the PC, console, cloud, or mobile entry for your device;
4. Sign in with the account used for launch activities;
5. Check the in-game mailbox and event panel for rewards.

Avoid unknown installers, unofficial “accelerated” download pages, and third-party redemption forms.

## Account syncing between platforms

The official announcement says that players who created a character on PC can continue on mobile with the same account. Confirm the account type and server information before signing in so you do not accidentally start a separate character.

## Why launch information can change

Early launch operations may include maintenance, store delays, staged mobile availability, or event rule changes. For a download, login, or reward issue, check the official announcements and in-game mailbox first, then record your platform, account region, and the exact error message.
