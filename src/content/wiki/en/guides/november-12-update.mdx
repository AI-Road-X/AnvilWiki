## When is Aniimo's November 12 maintenance?

Match your server to the timetable before planning a session. The official notice gives Europe, Americas and Apac separate local clocks. Its scheduled windows are shown below. These are announced times checked on November 11, ahead of the update. Recheck the notice if maintenance is extended. [Official update notice](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100298)

| Server | November 12 maintenance | Clock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Europe | 06:00–11:00 | UTC+0 |
| Americas | 02:00–07:00 | UTC−4 |
| Apac | 14:00–19:00 | UTC+8 |

Keep the server name beside any time you copy into a calendar. When comparing a friend's countdown with your own, first compare the UTC offsets. A time displayed by your device can differ from a fixed server clock, especially around a daylight-saving change. Use the in-game server label when you return to the client.

## Which activity should you inspect first?

Start with the activity your account can enter, then inspect its current objective and reward panel. The announcement gives Prismana Snapshots a **Student II** requirement and Aniimo Dash a **Pathfinder Level 40** requirement. Check the named rank and level separately. [Official activity requirements](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100298)

| Your immediate task | What to check after maintenance |
| --- | --- |
| Find an available activity | Read the entry requirement before planning a route or spending resources. |
| Continue a partly finished objective | Compare the objective text and reward counter with your own previous progress. |
| Join friends | Agree on server, mode and remaining session time before assembling a party. |
| Explore with limited play time | Pick one eligible activity and finish a clear objective before switching menus. |

For creature-specific questions, open the [compendium](/characters/) alongside the activity panel. A species page answers identity and mechanics questions. The event panel answers which target your current phase requests. Keeping both open is useful when a creature has several forms.

## What should you do before and after the update?

Before maintenance, record the active objective you want to resume and the rewards you have already claimed. After returning, check the client version and revisit that objective before following an older route. This makes a changed menu label, eligibility requirement or progress counter easier to spot.

1. Check the server and scheduled window above.
2. Leave a note of the quest or activity you intend to continue.
3. After maintenance, restart the client and read the current notices.
4. Inspect eligibility and progress before spending an activity item.
5. Claim completed rewards through the panel that lists them, then choose the next objective.

Use the [quest guide index](/quests/) when your blocker is an objective or menu step. Use the [map reference](/maps/official-region-names/) when it is a region name. Matching the guide to the exact blocker saves repeatedly retracing the same route.

## Does this update checklist establish a working reward code?

Check the reward's delivery method before looking for a place to paste a code. Open the announcement linked by the reward panel and follow its stated claim route. For code entry questions, the [redemption guide](/codes/how-to-redeem/) explains what to check. For an old event name, the [October event archive](/guides/october-2026-events/) preserves earlier schedules.
