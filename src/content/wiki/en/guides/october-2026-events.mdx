## Which Aniimo events still have published October dates?

As of **October 6, 2026**, the [official September 23 update notice](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100145) lists the following windows. The table uses the **Aniimo-Apac server clock (UTC+8)**; Europe and Americas use different local clocks in the same notice. These are announced schedules, not a guarantee that an event is currently accessible to every account. Confirm your server, eligibility, and any later changes in the game's event panel.

| Event | Announced Aniimo-Apac window (UTC+8) | Unlock condition | What to check first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aniimo Discovery | Sep 25, 04:00 – Oct 9, 03:59 | Student II | Whether free Search Attempts are available; the notice says unused free attempts do not stack. |
| Vein Abundance: Berylline Vale | Oct 5, 04:00 – Oct 12, 03:59 | Student II | Whether the event panel points to Berylline Vale for Prismana Form Waleetle. |
| Journey Chronicles | Oct 1, 04:00 – Oct 29, 03:59 | Student I | Your cumulative login progress and the in-game claim conditions. |
| Windchaser's Departure: Irisalis | Sep 25, 10:00 – Dec 10, 07:59 | Student I | The weekly-limited Iris Petal source and the current crafting requirements. |

The official notice also lists Europe and Americas dates. If your account is not on Aniimo-Apac, **do not copy the UTC+8 deadline into your own calendar**: open the source notice and use the row for your server. The current in-game display takes priority if a later update changes an event.

## What should you do first today?

1. Open the event panel and confirm your server and Student rank. An announced event may be locked even during its date window.
2. If Aniimo Discovery is available, check whether you have free Search Attempts before they replenish. The notice explicitly says unspent free attempts do not stack; it does **not** specify that every player receives the same number.
3. If you are looking for Prismana Form Waleetle, confirm that your panel shows **Berylline Vale**, then compare the creature details with the [Waleetle compendium](/characters/waleetle/) and the [map references](/maps/). The compendium describes the form; it does not guarantee a spawn on every visit.
4. Check Journey Chronicles for cumulative-login progress and any unclaimed rewards. For Irisalis, note the weekly petal limit before deciding how to spend petals; the official notice does not give a universal farming route.

This order prioritizes near-term windows and non-stacking attempts, not an untested claim about which activity gives the best rewards. If you are still learning the menus, use the [beginner guide](/guides/getting-started/) first.

## What already ended in the published schedule?

The same notice put **Glamour Star** through October 2 and the **Rosetower Woods** Vein Abundance phase through October 5 on the Aniimo-Apac clock. Do not treat those older entries as active merely because a launch-reward article still mentions them. A later rerun would need its own official or in-game confirmation.
