## Which Aniimo events are still scheduled to be active?

As of **October 14, 2026**, the [official September 23 update notice](https://aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100145) schedules two events beyond today's date. The notice gives separate local clocks for Europe, Americas, and Apac, so the table preserves each server's published window rather than converting them into one universal deadline. A published date is not a guarantee that an event is available to every account: confirm your server, unlock level, and the current event panel in-game.

| Scheduled event | Europe | Americas | Apac | Unlock condition / first check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Journey Chronicles | Oct 1, 04:00 – Oct 29, 03:59 UTC | Oct 1, 04:00 – Oct 29, 03:59 UTC−4 | Oct 1, 04:00 – Oct 29, 03:59 UTC+8 | Student I; check cumulative login progress and unclaimed rewards. |
| Windchaser's Departure: Irisalis | Sep 25, 10:00 – Dec 9, 23:59 UTC | Sep 25, 10:00 – Dec 9, 19:59 UTC−4 | Sep 25, 10:00 – Dec 10, 07:59 UTC+8 | Student I; check the weekly-limited Iris Petal source and current crafting requirements. |

The official [October 12 developer feedback post on Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4126040/allnews/) discusses planned game improvements, but does not replace the event schedule above. If a later announcement or your event panel differs, follow the newer official information shown for your server.

## What should you check first today?

1. Open the in-game event panel and confirm your server and Student rank. Use the exact server-specific time above; do not assume UTC+8 applies to your account.
2. Check Journey Chronicles for cumulative login progress, eligibility, and unclaimed rewards. The official notice describes login-day rewards but does not publish a universal claim status for individual accounts.
3. For Irisalis, check the event panel for the current weekly-limited Iris Petal source and crafting requirements before spending resources. Browse the [character compendium](/characters/) for related Aniimo references; the official event panel remains the source for live availability.
4. Use the [map references](/maps/) only after confirming that your in-game objective points to the same region. Do not follow an old Vein Abundance location as if that timed phase were still active.

This checklist is for checking published dates and avoiding missed claims; it is not a tested reward ranking or a promise of a specific drop. If you are still learning the menus, use the [beginner guide](/guides/getting-started/) first.
