## Where do you start with Aniimo maps?

Open the [Aniimo maps index](/maps/) and choose the reference that matches your question. Use [official region names](/maps/official-region-names/) when checking how a place is named, and the [region research guide](/maps/region-research/) for location notes. A name match alone does not confirm that a creature, item, or quest objective appears there.

## How should you read location claims?

Check whether a map note identifies its source and whether it is an official reference or a player observation. Treat unverified coordinates and encounter claims as leads to investigate, not guaranteed locations. If a location is linked to a quest, compare it with the relevant [quest guide](/quests/) rather than assuming every regional note describes a quest destination.

## What if a place name differs?

Check the official-name reference first, then compare alternate spellings in community notes. Keep the original wording visible when evidence is incomplete; do not silently convert an uncertain place name into an official one. You can cross-check nearby subjects in the [character compendium](/characters/) and [items index](/items/) without treating those pages as map evidence.
