## Region names shown on the official site

The map navigation on Aniimo's official website currently shows these English region names:

- Mistwoods
- Echoback Landing
- Sea of Flowers
- Blitzwood
- Nimbus Fields
- Forest of Falling Stars

These are the spellings currently shown by the official site. Community guides and player translations may use different localized names, so keep the English name alongside any translation.

## What is verified for Blitzwood?

The [official Blazen entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/046/basic-form) and [official Bolty entry](https://wiki.aniimo.com/item/045/mountain-woods-form) both list **Blitzwood** under Habitats. Our [Blazen guide](/characters/blazen/) and [Bolty guide](/characters/bolty/) explain their listed traits and skills. A habitat label is not a spawn coordinate or a guarantee that either Aniimo appears at every point in the region. The cited pages do not provide a route, rate, or exact location, so we do not publish one as fact.

## Why keep the official English spelling?

Localized names can change as the client, announcements, or community terminology develops. Keeping the original spelling helps readers search the official map, connect guides across languages, and avoid ambiguity in quest references.

## What to record in a region guide

For each map page, record the English region name, an official source link, related quests, verified resource locations, and the verification date. Mark untested locations as unverified instead of presenting a guessed route as fact.
