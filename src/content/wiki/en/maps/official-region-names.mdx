## What are Blitzwood and Mistwoods in Aniimo?

Blitzwood and Mistwoods are two of the named regions shown in Aniimo's official website navigation. The same official navigation currently shows these six English region names:

- Mistwoods
- Echoback Landing
- Sea of Flowers
- Blitzwood
- Nimbus Fields
- Forest of Falling Stars

These are the spellings currently shown by the official site. Community guides and player translations may use different localized names, so keep the English name alongside any translation. The table below gives source-linked examples of forms whose official habitat fields list Mistwoods or Blitzwood; it is not a complete spawn list.

A habitat label identifies a region associated with that form; by itself it does not give a spawn coordinate, route, time, appearance rate, or guarantee that an Aniimo appears at every point in the region. The cited entries do not provide those details, so they are not stated here as fact.

## Why keep the official English spelling?

Localized names can change as the client, announcements, or community terminology develops. Keeping the original spelling helps readers search the official map, connect guides across languages, and avoid ambiguity in quest references.

## What to record in a region guide

For each map page, record the official region name, the exact form shown in its habitat entry, a source link, related quests, separately verified resource locations, and the verification date. Mark untested locations as unverified instead of presenting a guessed route as fact.
