## What should a map note include?

Record the region, entry condition, target location, related quest, and verification version. A landmark, route, or time-of-day condition is more useful than a vague “nearby” description.

## Keeping map pages current

1. Confirm names with in-game text or official map information.
2. Add a verification date.
3. Recheck entrances, fast-travel points, and quest conditions after updates.
4. Mark untested locations as unverified instead of using absolute wording.

Link map notes to [quests](/en/quests/) and [items](/en/items/) so readers can follow a location to its objectives and rewards.
