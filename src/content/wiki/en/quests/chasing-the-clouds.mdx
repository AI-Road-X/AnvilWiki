## Why this quest matters

[PC Gamer's September 2026 redemption guide](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/aniimo-codes/) reports that completing **Chasing The Clouds** with Dr. Lewis unlocks the in-game mail menu used to claim code rewards. This quest-specific detail is third-party guidance, not an official quest rule. [Aniimo's launch announcement](https://www.aniimo.com/newslist/detail/100115) confirms that in-game rewards are delivered through official events and mail. If a redeemed reward is missing, first check the quest status and whether the mail menu is available in your current game version.

## Troubleshooting order

1. Check the quest list and confirm the quest is complete.
2. Return to the main menu and open the mailbox.
3. Change areas and check the mailbox again if delivery is delayed.
4. Record the code, time, and game version before checking official notices.
