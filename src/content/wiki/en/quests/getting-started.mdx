## How do you find an Aniimo quest?

Start at the [quest index](/quests/) and open the entry matching the quest name. Check its stated objective and any source or update information before following a step. If a detail is not documented, do not assume the quest has a particular prerequisite, location, or reward.

## How can you verify a quest step?

Compare location questions with the [maps index](/maps/) and resource questions with the [items index](/items/). Those references can provide context, but they do not independently prove a quest requirement. Keep official information distinct from player-reported observations when checking a step.

## What if the guide is incomplete?

Use only the steps the page supports, and recheck information that may depend on a game update. For a documented example, see [Chasing the Clouds](/quests/chasing-the-clouds/). To identify a character referenced by a quest, consult the [character compendium](/characters/) rather than inferring an identity from a partial name.
